Drift is a free web player built around synced lyrics. Bring your own files or play from YouTube, SoundCloud and Spotify, import playlists from a link, and keep a diary of the shows you've seen.

Open the player See what's inside

Lyrics that keep up

Word-by-word highlighting, tap a line to jump, nudge the timing if a file runs early or late. This is a demo loop.

Nothing to configure

Drift looks lyrics up for you, reads .lrc files you add yourself, and shows plain lyrics when no synced version exists.

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Synced lyrics

Line and word highlighting, per-track timing offsets, and a fullscreen view with a backdrop that moves with the music.

Playlist import

Paste a Spotify link to bring in a playlist or album, or a YouTube or SoundCloud link for a single track.

Listening rooms

Host a room for up to 50 people. Everyone follows your play, pause and seek, and can send reactions.

Music diary

Log gigs and festivals, rate albums, make lists, and see who else was there. Scan a poster to log a whole lineup.

Equalizer and speed

Five-band equalizer, playback speed, a sleep timer that fades out, and a queue you can reorder.

Your language

English and Georgian today, with a dictionary that's easy to extend.

Play from anywhere

Your own filesYouTubeSoundCloudSpotify

Streamed tracks play through each service's official embedded player, so they keep the service's own controls and terms. Spotify plays 30-second previews unless you're signed in to a Premium account in the same browser. The equalizer and audio-reactive visuals work with your own files.

Drift is an independent project. It isn't affiliated with Liner, YouTube, SoundCloud or Spotify, and those names belong to their owners. The player has no ads.
